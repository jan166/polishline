As your reading develops

Guide the translation

Carry choices about meaning and voice across the work. Keep an exception local without losing the broader distinction.

Try a scoped choice ↗How it works

Consistency can require different words.

Preserve the distinctions that carry the argument.

Consistency may mean keeping two concepts apart rather than rendering every occurrence identically.

Make an exception without losing the rule.

A personal habit is not the same as a social convention. Scope the different treatment to the occurrence that needs it.

Keep a voice, including where it changes.

Choices about register, rhythm and perspective can guide assistance alongside terminology.

Your choices have a reach.

A decision may concern one occurrence, a passage or a wider piece of work. Keep its scope visible so an exception does not become a blanket rule.

G

Guidance · scope

Suggestions are not instructions yet.

Extracted terms begin as Candidates. Only guidance you approve influences subsequent Draft, Polish, Ask and AI Review.

G

Guidance · approval

A choice can develop with the reading.

Reconsider a decision without erasing why you made it. Updating guidance does not silently rewrite text you have already worked on.

Whenever you reconsider

Revise with control

A later passage can change your reading. Revisit the guidance, inspect the consequences, and choose what happens to the text.

Reconsider a reading ↗How it works

Change your mind. Keep the account of it.

Review what an edit did to the meaning.

A Review note gives you something to consider. A separate Polish operation can propose a change.

Do not let old grounds dictate new work.

Reconsidered guidance stays distinct from earlier operation evidence. Accepted text is not silently replaced.

Revisit an interpretation—not just an old file.

Earlier grounds, changed guidance and alternative Editions support comparison without erasing the path you took.

Review does not take the pen.

An AI Review note identifies something to consider. It does not manufacture an edit. Use Polish when you want a proposed revision.

R

Review · separate from revision

Apply, dismiss or keep thinking.

Inspect the source, the proposed wording and the reason. Acceptance is a decision you make—not a side effect of receiving assistance.

Keep more than the latest wording.

Saved versions and alternative Editions preserve earlier lines of work. Revisit a choice without pretending the earlier one never happened.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the live product?

This is a guided, scripted illustration of Polishline’s intended working experience, not a miniature editor or a live AI session. The documents, questions and answers are prepared examples. Some illustrated capabilities may not be available in the workspace today.

Do I have to begin with an AI draft?

No. The intended workspace supports direct translation, source-led inquiry, drafting, guidance and revision as complementary ways of working. You can ask about a source passage before there is a translation, or translate yourself and use assistance only where it helps.

Does Ask change my text?

No. Ask returns answers and supports follow-up inquiry. It does not rewrite text or create a Suggestion. AI Review likewise returns notes, not AI-generated Suggestions. Polish is the separate operation for proposing a text revision.

Which file formats and exports should I expect?

The workspace handles text. The planned public launch includes DOCX round-tripping from Light and XLIFF, TMX and TBX interchange in Pro. These planned formats are not functions of this illustration.

Does it search the web or support shared projects?

The examples use supplied references, not live web research. This illustration does not search the web, create shared projects or demonstrate professional interchange.

Can an AI suggestion overwrite newer work?

A Suggestion records the wording and guidance it used. When those grounds change, it cannot simply overwrite newer work. The Revise example shows a reopened treatment, unchanged accepted text, and a separate new proposal that you can apply or dismiss.

What happens to text entered in this preview?

This illustration has no text-entry fields, AI calls, analytics or payment service. Its prepared examples run in your browser. Use Start free to open sign-in or account creation. Product privacy requirements are not an independent compliance audit.

Can I sign up or buy one of these plans?

The displayed prices and limits follow the planned public-launch catalog; they do not indicate current availability. Use Start free to open sign-in or account creation. This page does not collect payment details or start a subscription.

Can I replace a client-mandated CAT tool with it?

Do not assume that. When a client requires a particular CAT tool or delivery package, keep that requirement in view. This preview demonstrates Polishline’s working experience, not compatibility with a required production workflow.
